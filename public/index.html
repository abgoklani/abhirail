Copy & open IRCTC
  1. Log in to IRCTC (website or Rail Connect app).
  2. Type the From and To codes, pick the date, class and quota, then search.
  3. Find train and tap Book Now. Passengers can be picked from your IRCTC Master List.

IRCTC doesn't let other sites fill its booking form, so AbhiRail gives you the exact details to paste. Seat status can change before you book.

Missing a train?The railway data doesn't list every train. Add one by its number and AbhiRail checks its seats.

How to read the chance

Available = 100%A confirmed berth is open right now. Seats sell fast, so book soon. RAC means you can board and share a berth until one frees up.
Waitlist = estimateThe chance is a rule of thumb from the waitlist number: the lower the number, the higher the chance. It is not a measured prediction. Tatkal waitlists are marked down because they clear less often.
New trainsTrains launched in the last few weeks, such as the Vande Bharat Sleeper, may not be in the live data yet. They appear with a "New" tag and a link to check seats on IRCTC.
